# Sources and editorial scope

This is an independent English-language fan wiki for **Dice Hoarder**, developed and published by DieCorp Games. It is not an official developer website. The full game is listed on Steam as app **3687090**.

The first article set adapts source-based editorial research already maintained for the companion [dicehoarder.site](https://dicehoarder.site/). The dates shown by each article refer to its last editorial source review, not to a new gameplay test by this GitHub Pages project. The site-wide source review recorded here is November 19, 2026.

The disk names and edible-dice candidate locations are attributed community reports. They are not presented as official coordinates or independently tested routes. The article sources link to those discussions and guides directly.

## Content boundaries

- Suggestions such as a sorting order or search checklist are identified as editorial advice, not a measured fastest strategy.
- No complete die-design catalogue, ability unlock schedule, skill-disk coordinate map, crafting recipe, or achievement edge-case rule is invented.
- The store's Chill Mode and the launch announcement's Zen Mode are described as similar story-free options; available public sources do not establish them as separate menu choices.
- Historical patch notes are labeled by version. A change recorded in a dated announcement is not a guarantee that no later patch exists.
- Source review does not imply hands-on testing. Current availability and system requirements should be checked on the live Steam listing.

## Artwork and rights

The local gameplay screenshots in `public/assets/` are credited to DieCorp Games and remain their property. They identify and discuss the game; this repository does not claim ownership of the artwork. The asset `shop.webp` is based on an official Steam screenshot. Additional screenshot assets originated from the companion site's game-image collection.

## Maintenance

When new patches are announced, recheck affected mode, ability, controls, collectibles, and achievement claims against the original announcement. Update an article's review date only when its material has actually been checked. Keep patch publication dates separate from editorial review dates.
