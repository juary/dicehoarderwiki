A DIFFERENT KIND OF ADVENTURE

A place for every die.

Dice Hoarder is a first-person organizing game set in a mysterious dice shop. Sort dice by color and material, complete seven-piece sets, and uncover a story as you tidy up.

There are no timers, so take a breath and make the space your own.

questions.

Quick answers before you step inside.

More questions ↗
What is Dice Hoarder?

It's a single-player, first-person organizing game by DieCorp Games, set in a mysterious dice shop.

Is there a timer?

No. You can sort at your own pace. Story Mode uses a shelved-value goal to advance to the next day.

Where can I play it?

Dice Hoarder is available on Steam for Windows. Check the official store page for current availability and requirements.

Is this an official website?

No. This is an independent fan resource and is not affiliated with DieCorp Games.
